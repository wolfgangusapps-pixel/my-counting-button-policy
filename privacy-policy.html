Privacy Policy

My Counting Button does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information. The app's counter value exists only in memory on your device while the app is open and is not saved, uploaded, or shared with any third party.

The app requests no special device permissions and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If this policy changes in the future, an updated version will be posted at this same page.
