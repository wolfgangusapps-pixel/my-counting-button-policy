# Privacy Policy for Counting Button App

**Last Updated:** August 14, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how the Counting Button App ("App") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our application.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Button Click Data:** We collect counts of button presses and interactions while you use the App
- **User Preferences:** Settings and preferences you configure within the App
- **Crash and Error Reports:** If you choose to send us error reports

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, and version
- **App Usage Analytics:** How you interact with the App features
- **Performance Data:** App performance metrics and stability data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **App Functionality:** To provide and maintain the core counting button functionality
- **Improvements:** To enhance and optimize the App's performance and user experience
- **Bug Fixes:** To identify and resolve technical issues
- **Analytics:** To understand usage patterns and feature popularity
- **Support:** To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support

## 4. Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Counting data stored on your device is retained until you delete it
- **Server Data:** If applicable, we retain analytics data for up to 90 days
- **Deleted Accounts:** Upon request, we delete all associated data within 30 days

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** With vendors who assist us in operating the App (analytics, hosting)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, court order, or governmental request
- **Protection of Rights:** When necessary to protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property

## 6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure storage practices
- Regular security assessments
- Limited access to user data

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Our App may integrate with third-party services for:
- Analytics (e.g., Firebase, Mixpanel)
- Crash reporting
- Cloud storage

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies.

## 8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your data
- **Correction:** Correct inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your data
- **Portability:** Obtain your data in a portable format
- **Opt-Out:** Opt out of analytics or marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Counting Button App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

## 10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act and California Privacy Rights Act:

- Right to know what personal information is collected
- Right to know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Right to delete personal information collected
- Right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

## 11. GDPR Compliance (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area:

- We process your data based on your consent and legitimate business interests
- You have the right to access, rectify, erase, and restrict processing
- You have the right to data portability and to object to processing
- You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority
